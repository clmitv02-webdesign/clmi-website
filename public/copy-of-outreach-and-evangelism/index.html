Fun and Recreation
⁠Game Nights:Host regular game nights with a variety of games, such as board games, video games, or outdoor games.
Outdoor Activities:Plan outdoor activities, such as hiking, camping, or sports tournaments.
Movie Nights:Host movie nights with a discussion component, exploring themes and biblical connections.
Joy in Fellowship: We create spaces where youth can unwind, laugh, and build lasting friendships through wholesome, Christ-centered fun and recreational activities.
Faith in Everyday Moments: Whether around a game table, a campfire, or a movie screen, we help youth see God in the everyday—fostering joy, reflection, and deeper connection with others.
Balanced Spiritual Life: By integrating fun with faith, we encourage youth to live full, balanced lives—finding delight in God’s presence both in worship and in recreation.
