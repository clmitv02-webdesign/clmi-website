Children’s Ministry · CLMI

A place to know Jesus, grow in faith, and belong.

Every gathering is an invitation for children to discover God’s love, build lasting faith and enjoy meaningful moments with a caring church family.

Christ-centred experiences made for growing hearts.
Our programs

Three ways children grow with us.

Choose a program to see how CLMI helps children learn the Word, live out their faith and experience the joy of giving and receiving in community.

Faith begins with understanding

Sunday School

A welcoming time where Bible truth becomes clear, memorable and relevant to a child’s everyday world. Children are encouraged to ask questions, pray and discover what it means to walk with Jesus.

  • Bible stories made clear and engaging
  • Prayer, worship and joyful participation
  • Lessons children can carry into the week

Faith that reaches beyond Sunday

Children Evangelism

Children learn that sharing Christ’s love can begin with a kind word, a helping hand and the courage to tell someone what Jesus means to them. Faith becomes something they can live and share.

  • Simple ways to speak about Jesus
  • Kindness, service and compassion in action
  • Confidence to be a light wherever they go

Celebrating Jesus through generous love

Children’s Christmas Gift

A joyful seasonal experience that places the love of Jesus at the centre of Christmas. Through fellowship and thoughtful gifts, children are reminded that they are seen, valued and part of a caring community.

  • A Christ-centred Christmas celebration
  • Joyful fellowship and shared memories
  • Gifts offered with dignity, care and love

Every child has a place here.

Whether your family is visiting for the first time or looking for a church community where children can flourish in faith, we would love to welcome you and answer your questions.
