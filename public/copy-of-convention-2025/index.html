25th Annual Convention – A Life-Changing Experience!
The 25th Annual Convention held from May 1st to 3rd, 2026, at Christ's Love Ministries International was nothing short of an extraordinary blessing. Under the powerful theme "The Hand of God" (1 Kings 18:46), people from all corners of the globe gathered in faith, anticipation, and hope, and the atmosphere was filled with the power of healing, renewal, and divine transformation.
Over the three days, attendees experienced life-altering moments—healing, breakthroughs, and countless blessings. It was truly a time of restoration, as the hand of God moved mightily, touching hearts and lives in ways words can scarcely describe.
The convention brought together believers from all nations, united in the spirit, to receive God's Word and anointing in abundance. Powerful teachings, inspiring worship, and anointed prayers uplifted everyone in attendance, leaving us all with a renewed sense of purpose and faith.
For those who were able to attend, this event will be remembered for years to come as a highlight in their spiritual journey. And for those who missed it—don't worry, next year's Annual Convention promises to be even more extraordinary. You won't want to miss it!
Mark your calendars, invite your family and friends, and prepare for an unforgettable experience. The next convention will be a time of deeper healing, greater blessings, and a move of God like never before. Stay tuned, and get ready to witness what God has in store for His people in the year ahead!
